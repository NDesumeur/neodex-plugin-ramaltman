# Ram Altman

A survival horror game, and a parody. You are the night guard at **Octet & Fils**. Your job: keep **the Stack**, the country's last 64 GB of RAM, safe from midnight to 6 AM.

The problem is **Ram Altman**. The former CEO of ClosedRAM had himself replaced part by part, and now he wants only one thing: all the RAM in the world. Yours too. He never stops smiling.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

The game itself is in French.

## Controls

- **Mouse at the screen edges** (or **← →**): look left and right in the office.
- **Q / D**: close or open the left / right door. **A / E**: the left / right door light.
- **V**: the vent hatch. **Space**: raise or lower the camera tablet (or move the mouse over the bottom bar).
- **1 to 8**: pick a camera (or click the map). **C**: hang up the phone message. **Esc**: back to the menu.
- Every button in the office can be clicked too.

## The three threats

- **Ram Altman** walks from camera to camera up to your doors. In the dark you can't see him: turn on the light, and if he stands in the doorway, close it. If he finds an open door, he comes in: nothing responds any more, and he attacks as soon as you lower the tablet.
- Sometimes he stares into the camera. Sometimes, from night 3, you see him where he isn't.
- **The Scalper** sleeps at the loading dock (camera 5). He doesn't move while you watch him. Forget him and he runs down the east hall: right door open, he steals 16 GB; door closed, he bangs on it and it costs power.
- **The Chips** scratch inside the vent (camera 7). When they reach the hatch above you, close it, or they eat 8 GB.

- **The audio lure** (key **L**, or the button above the map) plays a chime in the room you are watching. If Ram is in a neighboring room, he comes to look: that's how you pull him away from a door. On camera 7, it drives the Chips back. It costs a little power and has to recharge.
- **Breakdowns** (from night 2): Ram sometimes hacks the cameras ("access denied"), the hatch jams, power leaks. The office monitor shows what is broken; you fix one thing at a time, in 4 seconds: **R** cameras, **T** hatch, **P** power (or click the monitor line).
- **Gérard** (from night 4), Léo's father, walks into the office if you stay on the cameras too long. Lower the tablet to make him leave, or he takes a stick "for Léo".
- **Your ears**: Ram's steps are louder when he is close, left or right, and nearby room mics flag them on the map. But from night 4, he imitates the chime and his own steps in another room.
- **The phone** sometimes rings in the middle of the night: it's Léo. He tells you where Ram is… and doesn't always tell the truth.

Everything uses power: doors, lights, hatch, cameras. At 0%, everything goes dark… and Ram comes humming.

## The story

Why is the camera clock stuck on November 13, 1987? Who was Léo Octet? Papers sometimes lie on the cameras for a few seconds: click them to pick them up. Every night you win also adds a newspaper clipping. Everything can be reread in the **Archives** from the menu, and whoever finds all 8 fragments and survives night 6 gets the true ending. Two of the fragments are tapes you can listen to.

## Nights

- Night 1 starts with a short tutorial with arrows (replay it from Settings).

- 6 nights, each harder, each with its phone message (subtitled, and read aloud if your system has a French voice).
- **Custom night**: set each threat from 0 to 20, and add challenges (no lure, 50% power, slow tablet). A challenge won with every threat at 10 or more earns a medal.
- **Night 7**: hidden, it unlocks after the true ending. This time you are Léo, and the guard is looking for you on the cameras.
- **Three stars**: beat night 5, night 6, then the custom night at 20 / 20 / 20.

## Data and sync

The night reached, the stars, the documents found and the custom night settings are saved by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs if sync is on.

## Good to know

Jumpscares, screams and sudden noises. In **Settings**: sound and voice volume, mute, look speed, and softened jumpscares (no scream, no hallucinations).

It's a parody: no real CEO was robotized. Everything is painted with the canvas brush (no images) and every sound is made live.
